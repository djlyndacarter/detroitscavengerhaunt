The Haunt

You aren't watching the horror. You're inside it.

Motor City Massacre doesn't keep the horror safely inside a building — we take it to the streets. The lights of Downtown Detroit, darkened corners, alleys, shadows, and people who seem to be watching you a little too closely.

Eerie figures lurk throughout the experience. Some may help you. Some may have your next clue. And some are there for an entirely different reason.
